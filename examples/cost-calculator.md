# AI Spend Calculator

A free calculator for estimating monthly AI cost, cost per customer, and margin risk.

## How to use

Open `examples/cost-calculator.html` in a browser and enter:

- model price preset
- requests per day
- average input tokens per request
- average output tokens per request
- active customers
- monthly revenue per customer

## Why this exists

Most AI teams do not feel the cost problem until the bill arrives. This calculator creates the first decision moment:

1. Estimate monthly AI cost.
2. Compare AI cost against revenue.
3. Identify margin risk.
4. Move from estimates to real tracking with AIProfitHub Cloud.
